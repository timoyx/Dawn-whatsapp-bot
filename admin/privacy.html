Privacy Policy – Dawn Jacar WhatsApp Assistant

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Dawn Jacar ("we", "us") runs a WhatsApp assistant that answers customer questions about our car-care services and passes conversations to our staff when needed. This policy explains what we collect through WhatsApp and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The messages you send us and our replies, with their dates and times.
  • Photos, files or locations only if you choose to send them.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and help with bookings and services.
  • To let our staff see the conversation and reply to you personally.
  • To improve the information our assistant gives.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) delivers messages between you and us.
  • Supabase stores the conversation history on servers in Singapore.
  • Anthropic (Claude) generates the assistant's replies from your message and our business information.

These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation history for up to 24 months so we can follow up on bookings and service questions, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you or delete your conversation history at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp and asking for "delete my data". You can stop receiving messages by blocking our number.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message us on WhatsApp.
